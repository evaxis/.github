# Support

Evaxis is in the foundation/design stage, not a supported production service.
No availability, compatibility or support SLA is offered.

Use an issue in the appropriate repository for non-sensitive questions:

- [Core scope and planned contracts](https://github.com/evaxis/evaxis/issues)
- [Architecture and roadmap](https://github.com/evaxis/architecture/issues)
- [Organisation documentation](https://github.com/evaxis/.github/issues)

Identify whether a statement is Confirmed, Proposed or Undecided. Do not upload
customer evidence, credentials, tenant identifiers or sensitive findings.
Use [private security reporting](SECURITY.md) for suspected vulnerabilities.

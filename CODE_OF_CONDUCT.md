# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique ideas and evidence, not
people. Make technical disagreements specific, constructive and factual.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of
private information and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not
publish sensitive security findings or customer data.

These expectations apply to Evaxis issues, pull requests and other
project-managed spaces. Maintainers may moderate content or restrict
participation proportionately, explain decisions where safe and protect the
privacy of people reporting concerns.

For a conduct concern, contact the owner through
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/goldjg) to arrange a private channel.
Do not publish another person's private information to make a report.
For vulnerabilities, follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
